Case studies

ICPM capstone

NutriChef

Business Model Canvas  ·  Institute of Product Leadership  ·  Certification capstone

Problem

Fitness-goal users and people managing conditions like diabetes or heart disease both need consistent, trustworthy home-cooked food — but food-delivery platforms don't verify who's cooking or track output against a health goal.

Business model

Designed a two-sided model connecting verified, trained chefs to customers, monetized through tiered monthly subscriptions (₹4,000–₹15,000) plus chef service fees, with secondary revenue from dietician consultations and corporate wellness contracts.

Key decision

The two segments needed different relationship models — self-serve subscriptions with progress tracking for fitness users, versus high-touch dietician support for medical users — which drove distinct channel and partnership strategy: gyms and corporate wellness for one, clinics and dieticians for the other.

Internal tool

Prospect Intelligence Tool

React / Vite  ·  Built for Locobuzz's own sales team  ·  2026

Problem

Our sales team was manually digging through LinkedIn profiles to figure out a prospect's goals and role objectives before outreach — slow, and inconsistent between reps.

What I built

A tool that surfaces a company's goals, an individual's role objectives, and industry context, aligned to what Locobuzz actually sells — so outreach starts from relevance, not a cold guess.

Where it's at

In active iteration — currently working through CORS restrictions and profile-reading accuracy. Being built and tested against real outreach, not a demo.
